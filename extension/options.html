Connect the extension

Paste your personal token from the Readbox web app. The extension will then save pages and highlights to your library.

Backend URL
Where your Readbox is hosted. Leave the default if you use the standard Emergent preview URL.
Personal Token
In Readbox, click the "Extension" button in the top-right header to reveal your token.
How to get your token
  1. Open your Readbox web app.
  2. Click the Extension button in the top navigation.
  3. Copy the token shown.
  4. Paste it above and click Save & Test.
